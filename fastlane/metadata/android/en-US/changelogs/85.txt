Tubik v1.2.1:

- Fixed many videos failing with "This video is not available" in release builds. A compiler bug corrupted an internal string used to detect and recover from unplayable responses, so the fallback clients never ran.
- Release builds now log a clear warning, and show a clear error, when the APK signature doesn't match the official certificate.
- Added diagnostic logging for player requests (no tokens or personal data).

Tubik v1.2.2:

- Turning on Music Removal during playback no longer lets the original audio through: speech now fades in from silence, and stays silent if it can't be processed.
- When Music Removal is muted because the device can't keep up, the audio is now fully silent, and speech fades back in smoothly once it recovers.
- The last words of a video are no longer cut off at the end with Music Removal on.
- Website updates now always keep the current release's download link, version and checksum.
